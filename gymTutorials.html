Machine Tutorials

Learn how to use our gym machines safely and effectively with these step-by-step video guides.

Treadmill Tutorial

Great for cardiovascular exercise!

How to use: Step on the side rails before starting. Begin with a slow speed and gradually increase. Always attach the safety clip. Cool down for 5 minutes before stopping.

Elliptical Tutorial

Perfect for low-impact cardio.

How to use: Step on the pedals and grip the handles. Keep your back straight. Push and pull the handles in time with your legs. Start at a low resistance and build up gradually.

Leg Press Machine Tutorial

Build quad and glute strength.

How to use: Set the seat so knees form a 90 degree angle. Place feet shoulder-width apart. Push through your heels. Do not lock out your knees at the top.

Rowing Machine Tutorial

Full body cardio workout.

How to use: Strap your feet in. Sit tall with a straight back. Push with your legs first, then pull the handle to your lower ribs. Return arms first, then lean forward, then bend knees.

Exercise Tutorials

Learn proper techniques for common exercises with these instructional videos.

Squat Tutorial

The best lower body exercise.

How to do it: Rest the bar on your upper traps. Feet shoulder width apart, toes slightly out. Brace your core and squat down until hips are below knees. Drive through your heels to stand.

Bench Press Tutorial

Builds chest, shoulder and tricep strength.

How to do it: Lie flat on the bench with eyes under the bar. Grip slightly wider than shoulder width. Lower the bar slowly to your mid chest. Press back up and lock out at the top.

Deadlift Tutorial

Works almost every muscle in your body.

How to do it: Stand with feet hip width apart, bar over mid foot. Hinge at hips and grip the bar. Brace your core. Drive feet into the floor and keep the bar close to your body.

Pull Ups Tutorial

Builds back and bicep strength.

How to do it: Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width. Hang at full extension. Pull your chin above the bar by driving elbows down. Lower slowly taking 2 seconds on the way down.
